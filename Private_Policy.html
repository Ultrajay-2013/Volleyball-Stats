Privacy Policy

Volleyball Stats · Effective October 24, 2026

The short version

  • Your game data is stored on your device and, if you sign in, in a private folder in your own Google Drive. We don't run a server, so we never see it.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics.
  • Data is only sent to Groq if you choose to use the optional AI Insights feature.
  • You can delete your data at any time.

About this app

Volleyball Stats is a web app for tracking volleyball statistics from the sideline. In this policy, "the app," "we" and "us" mean Volleyball Stats and its developer, and "you" means anyone using the app.

Information the app collects

When you sign in with Google (optional)

If you choose to sign in, Google shares the following with the app:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your profile picture
  • a Google account ID, used only to keep your data separate from other accounts on the same device

The app uses this information only to show who is signed in and to save and sync your data. You can use the app without signing in.

Game information you enter

  • team names
  • player names and jersey numbers
  • the plays you record and the statistics calculated from them
  • saved rosters and past games
  • AI insights you generate

Player names are entered by the coach or team staff member using the app. If you prefer, you can use first names, nicknames or jersey numbers only.

What the app does not collect

The app does not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers, payment information or browsing activity. It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

Where your data is stored

  • On your device. Your games, history and rosters are saved in your web browser's local storage on the device you're using.
  • In your Google Drive (only if you sign in). Your data is saved in a hidden, private app data folder in your own Google Drive so it can sync across your devices. Only this app can read that folder. It doesn't appear with your other Drive files, and the app cannot see or access any of your other files.

The app has no server or database of its own. The developer does not receive, store or have access to your game data or your Google account information.

How your data is shared

We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Data leaves your device only in these cases:

  • Google. Sign-in is handled by Google, and synced data is stored in your Google Drive. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Groq (optional AI Insights). If you tap Generate Insights, the app sends that game's team names, player names, jersey numbers and statistics to Groq to create coaching insights. This happens only when you tap the button, and it uses a Groq API key that you provide. Your Groq API key is stored only on your device and is sent only to Groq. Groq's handling of that data is covered by its own privacy policy. If you don't use AI Insights, nothing is sent to Groq.

Google user data

The app requests only the Google permissions it needs: your basic profile (name, email and picture) and access to its own private app data folder in your Google Drive. It uses data received from Google APIs only to provide sign-in and sync for you. That data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not transferred to anyone else except as described above. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Individual games and rosters: delete them in the app (Game History → Select or Delete Game, and Load Roster → ×). If you're signed in, the deletion syncs to your other devices.
  • Data on a device: signing out removes your account's data from that device. To remove signed-out data, clear the site's data in your browser settings.
  • Data in your Google Drive: open Google Drive on a computer, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Volleyball Stats, open its Options menu, and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • Stop the app's access to your Google account: visit myaccount.google.com/linkedapps and remove Volleyball Stats.

Because the developer never holds copies of your data, deleting it in these places removes it from the app completely. Data already sent to Groq for AI Insights is governed by Groq's policies.

Security

The app is served over HTTPS, and Google handles sign-in, so the app never sees your Google password. Your synced data is protected by your Google account. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so please keep your Google account and devices secure.

Who the app is for

The app is meant for coaches, players and team staff. To sign in, you must meet Google's minimum age requirements for a Google Account in your country. The app is not designed to collect information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. If you keep using the app after a change, the updated policy applies to you.
