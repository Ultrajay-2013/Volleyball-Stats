How to Use

Quick start

  1. Enter your team name and the opponent's name.
  2. Add your players (jersey number and name), or tap Load Roster to use a saved one.
  3. Tap Start Game.
  4. After each play, tap what happened, then tap the player who did it.
  5. When the match is over, tap Game Over to see the full summary.

Recording plays

  • Buttons are grouped by action (Serve, Serve Receive, Receive, Set, Attack, Block, Free Ball, Miscommunication, Opponent). Green buttons are good plays, and the ones that score give your team the point. Red buttons are bad plays, and the ones that score give the opponent the point.
  • After tapping a play, tap the player (or several players, such as a double block) in the pop-up, then tap Done. Tap a player again to unselect them. Every chosen player gets the stat.
  • End of Rally appears for plays that don't always end the rally. Turn it on when the play won or lost the point. It turns off again for the next play.
  • Undo removes the last play, even if it ended a set.

Which plays score

Sets & scoring

  • Matches are best of 3 sets.
  • Sets 1 and 2 go to 25 and set 3 goes to 15. You have to win by 2.
  • When a set is won, you can start the next set or undo the last play if it was a mistake.

After the game

  • The summary shows the score by set, how points were won and lost, and every player's stats.
  • Player stats have one table per action (Serve, Receive, Set, Attack and so on), showing good and bad plays split into ones that scored and ones that didn't, plus the net points and the net of all plays.
  • Back to Game lets you fix mistakes. New Game saves the game to Game History and keeps your roster.
  • Open Game History from the setup screen to look at past games. To delete several at once, tap Select (bottom right), tap the games (or Select All), then tap Delete.

Saved rosters

  • Save Roster stores your team name and players under a name you choose. Saving with the same name updates it.
  • Load Roster fills them back in. Tap × next to a roster to delete it.

AI insights

  1. Create a free API key at console.groq.com/keys.
  2. On any game summary, tap Generate Insights and paste in the key. You only have to do this once.
  3. You get 3 coaching insights about the game. Regenerate gets a fresh set.

To try insights without playing a real match, tap Simulate Game on the setup screen. It fills a full match with random plays using your roster (or sample players if it's empty) and opens the summary. Simulated games are never saved.

Insights need internet. Team names, player names and stats are sent to Groq to create them.

Sign in with Google

  • Tap Sign in with Google at the top of the setup screen to save your current game, history and rosters in your Google account and use them on any device.
  • Your data goes in a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Only this app can read it, and it doesn't count as a file you can see or share.
  • The app still works offline. Changes are saved on the phone and sync when you're back online.
  • During a game, a small pill at the top shows sync status (the setup screen shows it in the account box). Google sign-in lasts about an hour. When the pill says Reconnect, tap it to keep syncing (one tap, no password). Your plays are saved on the phone the whole time.
  • Signing out removes your account's data from this device. It stays in your Google account.
  • Sign-in only works when the app is opened from its web address (like GitHub Pages) and the Google Client ID has been added. See google-sync-setup.md.

Your data

  • Signed out, games, history and rosters are saved in this browser on this device, and they stay there if you close the page. Clearing your browser data erases them.
  • Signed in, they're also saved in your Google account and sync across devices.
  • Your Groq API key always stays on this device and is never synced.
